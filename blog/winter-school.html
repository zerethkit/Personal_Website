My experience in Winter School

Christmas and New Year in Seoul, calculus in the mornings, and the rest of the day to myself.

At the end of 2025, I spent two and a half weeks at Korea University's winter school in Seoul, taking Calculus. The programme ran from 23 December to 9 January, so I spent both Christmas and New Year in Korea.

I booked two separate flights, Singapore to Jeju and then a domestic flight from Jeju to Seoul. It was cheaper, and I got to spend 2 days in Jeju. The weather was perfect at 15 degrees, so I did some running. Then I flew to Seoul, which was freezing.

Classes ran from 9am to 12pm, which left most of every day free.

On Christmas Day I went to Nami Island, a tree-lined island about an hour and a half from Seoul. It is known for the avenues of trees running down it, and as a filming location for Korean dramas. Most people take the ferry across, but I took the zipline instead. It was -10 that day, so that was a mistake.

Two days later I went to Lotte World, a huge theme park that is mostly indoors, which is what you want in a Seoul winter. On 4 January I went skiing at Elysian Gangchon, which isn't something you get to do in Singapore.
